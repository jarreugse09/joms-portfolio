# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, static export, deployed on Vercel. Frontend only: no backend, database, or auth (user-specified).

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers screening for junior / entry-level web or AI roles. They skim fast, often on a laptop between other tabs, and need to find who Jomarie is, what he has shipped, and how to reach him within a minute. Secondary: developers who will click into the live sites and repos and notice craft.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Jomarie D. Esguerra, a graduating 4th-year Computer Science student at TUP Manila (Philippines). It proves he can build interactive, production-quality web experiences and has shipped real client and AI projects. Success: a recruiter leaves with his name, his strongest projects, and a contact action; a developer leaves remembering the site.

## Positioning

A student developer who has already shipped client websites (RMB Law, Setmona, BYD Alabang) and AI tools (Gemini chatbots, YOLO face detection), and whose own site is itself the strongest demonstration of interactive craft.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, resumes, and job applications. They compare against other junior portfolios. Projects link to live Vercel deployments and GitHub repos.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections: Hero, About, Experience, Skills/Tech Stack, Featured Projects, Other Projects, Contact.
- Experience = education (TUP Manila, BS Computer Science, graduating) + client work drawn from existing portfolio. No invented titles or dates; dates live in one data file for the owner to fill.
- Signature interactive pieces required by the owner: a cursor-tracking eye character, an original pixel-art ship, and a working retro portable music player with locally hosted, properly licensed audio (no audible autoplay).
- Must stay fast, accessible, and readable with motion reduced.

## Product Principles

1. Recruiter clarity first: every playful element sits beside content that is findable in seconds.
2. Show, don't claim: real screenshots, real links, real stack.
3. The site is the portfolio piece: interactions must be genuinely well built, not decoration.
4. Honest content: unknown facts are marked for the owner, never invented.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA target. Full keyboard access to the music player and navigation; prefers-reduced-motion respected; no autoplaying audio.
